About Me

Crafting spaces with intention

I am a licensed architect with a passion for creating spaces that honour both function and beauty. My work spans residential design, commercial interiors, and urban planning — always rooted in deep respect for context, culture, and the people who inhabit the spaces.

With a dual practice in architecture and interior design, I approach every project as a complete narrative — from the first structural concept to the final material selection.
